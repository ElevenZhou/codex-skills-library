# Direction Packs

Use these as starting coordinates, not templates. Adapt one pack to the domain and existing brand. Do not combine several packs by default.

| Direction | Best for | Silhouette | Typography | Color and material | Motion | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Minimal Civic | Long-session B2B, civic tools, knowledge work | Ledger, ruled spread, quiet workspace | Editorial serif plus precise sans | Warm white, ink, muted civic accents, fine rules | Stillness and subtle continuity | Can become anonymous without a signature |
| Industrial Command | Incident response, infrastructure, monitoring | Console, rail, dense grid, command queue | Condensed display plus mono data | Black/bone, warning red, acid or teal signals, hard borders | Snap and immediate state feedback | High visual pressure during routine work |
| Cartographic Night | Transport, logistics, city governance, geospatial tools | Full-bleed map plus field ledger | Editorial display plus utilitarian labels | Cartographic ink, route colors, generated or real map material | Spatial continuity and focused reveals | Asset weight and overlay contrast |
| Editorial Authority | Research, publishing, strategy, premium services | Spread, columns, image-led sequence | Strong serif display and refined body | Paper, ink, selective photography, sharp accent | Measured reveals | Editorial decoration can weaken task hierarchy |
| Technical Instrument | Science, audio/video, engineering, telemetry | Split pane, meters, scales, instrument field | Technical display and mono numerals | Functional neutrals, calibrated state colors | Continuous direct manipulation | Fake HUD elements without function |
| Humanist Service | Healthcare, support, civic service, nonprofit | Guided steps, calm grouping, readable measures | Highly legible humanist type | Reassuring neutrals with accessible status colors | Gentle progress and confirmation | Can become generic or patronizing |
| Direct Brutalism | Events, studios, experimental products, youth culture | Exposed grid, hard cuts, oversized type | Heavy display plus neutral utility type | Black/white, abrupt accent, raw rules | Snappy cuts | Random harshness without product rationale |
| Quiet Precision | Finance, analytics, professional tools | Tight alignment, data tables, precise whitespace | Refined sans with strong numerals | Neutral field, one restrained accent, fine borders | Subtle continuity | Excessive whitespace can slow repeated action |

## Selection Heuristics

- Choose `Minimal Civic` when cognitive endurance matters more than visual spectacle.
- Choose `Industrial Command` when time-to-recognition and severity handling dominate.
- Choose `Cartographic Night` only when spatial relationships are central to the job.
- Choose `Editorial Authority` when content sequence and authorship carry trust.
- Choose `Technical Instrument` when users directly manipulate measurements or systems.
- Choose `Humanist Service` when reassurance, clarity, and accessibility are primary.
- Choose `Direct Brutalism` when deliberate cultural positioning justifies the friction.
- Choose `Quiet Precision` for repeated analysis and comparison with restrained branding.

## Anti-Repeat Test

Compare with recent work when available. Change at least three of:

- page silhouette
- type category
- dominant neutral temperature
- accent role
- border and radius language
- image treatment
- navigation model
- motion grammar

Reject a direction if the product could be replaced with an unrelated logo without changing the design logic.
