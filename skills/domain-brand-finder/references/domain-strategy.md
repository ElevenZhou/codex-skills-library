# Domain Strategy Reference

## Candidate scoring rubric

Score each available candidate from 1-5:

- Brand fit: matches product category and audience.
- Memorability: easy to remember after one exposure.
- Spelling: low risk of misspelling when heard aloud.
- Trust: does not sound like spam, arbitrage, or a temporary tool.
- Expandability: supports future products beyond the current feature set.
- TLD quality: `.com` strongest by default; `.ai` strong for AI-native products; others context-dependent.
- Risk: low trademark collision, negative meaning, pronunciation, or regional misunderstanding risk.

## Naming lanes

Use multiple lanes instead of one flat brainstorm:

- Descriptive: clear category terms, e.g. model, flow, gate, stack.
- Coined: invented but pronounceable, e.g. FlaiOS.
- Company-linked: derived from existing entity, e.g. TimenovAI.
- Infrastructure-grade: control, core, grid, mesh, vault, nexus, orbit.
- Benefit-led: clarity, cost, control, speed, trust.
- Local-language: Chinese/English pairings if the product needs Chinese market use.

## Legal and operational caveats

- Domain availability is not trademark clearance.
- RDAP availability is not a purchase guarantee.
- Registrar carts are not ownership until payment completes.
- Hosting location and ICP filing are separate from where the domain is purchased.
- For China mainland hosting, ICP filing can be required; for non-mainland hosting, it usually is not.
